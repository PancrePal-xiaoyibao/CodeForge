# Finance and investment opportunity patterns

## High-value recurring clusters

| Cluster | Evidence signals | Likely reusable contents | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company and industry research | Repeated filings, KPI extraction, peer comparisons | source hierarchy, KPI schema, peer template | figures reconcile to dated primary sources |
| Valuation and scenario analysis | Reused DCF, multiples, sensitivities | model template, assumption register, validators | formulas, units and scenarios reconcile |
| Credit and counterparty review | Recurring covenant, liquidity, leverage analysis | credit rubric, covenant schema, red flags | calculations trace to source documents |
| Portfolio or exposure reporting | Repeated aggregation and commentary | exposure schema, attribution template | totals reconcile and timestamps are explicit |
| Treasury and liquidity planning | Recurring cash forecasts and funding decisions | cash model, scenario playbook, controls | opening-to-closing cash bridge reconciles |
| Market or event monitoring | Repeated news, filings, policy and price synthesis | event schema, source ladder, freshness rules | current state is separated from historical narrative |

## Good skill boundaries

Package research method, model controls, and reporting—not a standing promise of financial advice. Separate public-market, private-market, credit, treasury, and personal-finance workflows when sources and duties differ.

## Candidate evidence

Look for repeated manual KPI extraction, copied model tabs, recurring source reconciliation, recurring memo structures, or decisions delayed by stale data.

## Risk controls

Timestamp market data, identify currency and units, distinguish reported from adjusted figures, preserve assumptions, and use primary sources for material claims. Require qualified human review for investment, lending, suitability, tax, treasury, or regulated advice.
