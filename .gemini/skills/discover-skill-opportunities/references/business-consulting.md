# Business consulting opportunity patterns

## High-value recurring clusters

| Cluster | Evidence signals | Likely reusable contents | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem framing and workplan design | Repeated issue trees, hypotheses, interview plans | framing rubric, workplan templates, sector references | questions are mutually distinct and decision-linked |
| Market and competitor assessment | Same source plan, sizing logic, comparison dimensions | research protocol, source hierarchy, sizing model | assumptions and sources are traceable |
| Operating model and process redesign | Recurring stakeholder maps, RACI, bottleneck analysis | process schema, interview guide, control checklist | future state has owners and measurable controls |
| Executive decision memo | Repeated synthesis into options and recommendations | memo structure, evidence rubric, decision table | recommendation maps to evidence and tradeoffs |
| Due diligence and risk review | Similar request lists, red flags, evidence rooms | diligence checklist, red-flag taxonomy | every conclusion links to evidence or gap |
| Meeting-to-action conversion | Recurring synthesis, follow-ups, decision logs | action schema, decision log, follow-up template | owners, dates, dependencies, decisions captured |

## Good skill boundaries

Package a repeatable advisory method and output contract, not generic "be a consultant" roleplay. Separate industry-specific knowledge into references. Keep client-specific facts outside the reusable skill unless access and retention are approved.

## Candidate evidence

Look for repeated slide structures, copied interview questions, recurring stakeholder objections, recurring requests for the same analysis, and manual synthesis across documents.

## Risk controls

Separate facts, assumptions, estimates, and recommendations. Record source dates and conflicts. Require human judgment for organizational, employment, pricing, negotiation, or reputational decisions.
