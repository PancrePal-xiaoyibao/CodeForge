# Product and operations opportunity patterns

## High-value recurring clusters

| Cluster | Evidence signals | Likely reusable contents | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voice-of-customer synthesis | Repeated interview, survey, review, and ticket coding | taxonomy, extraction schema, evidence matrix | themes trace to source samples |
| Prioritization and roadmap decisions | Recurring scoring and stakeholder reconciliation | decision rubric, dependency map, roadmap template | priorities expose assumptions and tradeoffs |
| Requirements and acceptance criteria | Repeated ambiguity resolution and edge-case review | requirement schema, question bank, test template | requirements are testable and owned |
| Launch planning | Recurring cross-functional checklist and readiness review | launch gates, RACI, rollback plan | owners, dependencies and go/no-go criteria complete |
| Growth or content experiments | Repeated briefs, variants, tracking and readouts | experiment template, metric dictionary, analysis rules | hypotheses and stopping rules predeclared |
| Support and operational triage | Recurring classification, routing and escalation | taxonomy, SLA rules, response templates | correct owner, urgency and closure evidence |

## Good skill boundaries

Package a decision process plus shared schemas and gates. Keep product-specific facts, customer data, and current roadmap in controlled references or source systems. Prefer workflow automation for simple routing without meaningful reasoning.

## Candidate evidence

Look for recurring clarification meetings, duplicated launch checklists, inconsistent metric definitions, repeated ticket tagging, or decisions revisited because assumptions were not recorded.

## Risk controls

Separate user evidence from stakeholder opinion, declare metric definitions, prevent dark patterns, respect consent and privacy, and require owners for irreversible launches or customer communication.
