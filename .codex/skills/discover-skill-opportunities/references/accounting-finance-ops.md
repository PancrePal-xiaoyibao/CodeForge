# Accounting and finance operations opportunity patterns

## High-value recurring clusters

| Cluster | Evidence signals | Likely reusable contents | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transaction classification and reconciliation | Repeated mapping, unmatched items, manual tie-outs | chart-of-accounts rules, matcher, exception queue | opening + movements = closing; unmatched items listed |
| Period close | Recurring checklist, dependencies, late evidence | close calendar, ownership matrix, evidence pack | all accounts and approvals complete |
| Management reporting | Repeated extracts, variance commentary, packs | metric dictionary, report template, variance rules | source-to-report totals reconcile |
| Accounts payable or receivable | Recurring invoice checks, aging, collections | validation rules, workflow states, communication templates | subledger and general ledger reconcile |
| Expense and revenue controls | Repeated policy checks and exceptions | policy reference, evidence checklist, exception log | sampled transactions meet control criteria |
| Audit and tax support | Repeated schedules, request tracking, evidence retrieval | PBC tracker, workpaper templates, cross-reference rules | schedules tie to books and source evidence |

## Good skill boundaries

Use skills to orchestrate classification, evidence gathering, exception handling, review, and reporting. Use deterministic accounting software or scripts for postings and calculations. Keep entity-specific chart of accounts, policies, periods, and materiality in controlled references.

## Candidate evidence

Look for repeated spreadsheet formulas, manual matching, recurring close delays, inconsistent variance explanations, repeated auditor requests, or control evidence assembled from multiple systems.

## Risk controls

Preserve audit trails, periods, entity, currency, tax treatment, approvals, segregation of duties, and immutable source evidence. Never post entries, submit filings, initiate payments, or certify reports without explicit authorization and qualified review.
